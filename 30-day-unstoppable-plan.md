# THE 30-DAY UNSTOPPABLE PLAN
### Build momentum, visibility, and discipline — starting now.

**Reality check:** You cannot master everything in 30 days. You CAN become unrecognizably more disciplined, visible, and skilled in one specific thing. That is the whole strategy.

## The Strategy (Napoleon's Rule Applied)

**Your ONE primary focus:** Build an online presence as a "coder who trains." (Coding + fitness/running is a real, relatable niche: discipline meets craft.)

**Your ONE backup skill:** Coding. Practice it daily — it is your content fuel AND your career skill.

**What gets sacrificed:** None of your free time is wasted, but football becomes recreation, not a goal. Everything funnels into the mission.

## The Daily System (1.5 hours)

| Time | Block | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 30 min | **LEARN** | Study coding (tutorial / build something small) OR research your niche |
| 30 min | **CREATE** | Write one post / short essay / reel about what you're learning. Always show your own progress — people follow journeymen, not masters |
| 20 min | **ENGAGE** | Comment on 5–10 posts in your niche + send 1–2 thoughtful DMs/messages to people you admire |
| 10 min | **TRAIN** | Body follows mind: pushups, run, or gym. Non-negotiable. |

**+ 2 minutes:** Update your Franklin scorecard (below).

## The 30 Days in Phases

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Days 1–3)
- Pick your exact niche: "**X coding on the road to discipline**" (e.g., "Building skills in public — code every day, train every day").
- Set up/optimize 2 platforms: one you can write on (X/LinkedIn/Instagram) and one you can build on (GitHub or a blog).
- Write your bio: "I'm learning [skill] in public. Following along for the process."
- Make the calendar: same 90 minutes, same time, every day.

### Phase 2 — Build the Engine (Days 4–10)
- Post EVERY day. One useful idea, one progress update.
- Finish one small coding project (a script, a page, a bot — anything public).
- Steal the pattern: what are the top 5 accounts in your niche posting? Study structure, never copy words.
- Day 7: reflect in your scorecard — what got the most response?

### Phase 3 — Compound (Days 11–20)
- Double down on the content type that performed best.
- Publish your first "big" thing: a short essay or a walkthrough of your project.
- Reach out to 3–5 people in the niche with genuine questions (not "please promote me").
- Expect silence. Silence is normal. Roosevelt's arena: keep going.

### Phase 4 — Momentum & Proof (Days 21–30)
- Pick your single best piece. Improve it. Repost it at the best time.
- Finish a capstone: a project + a post that tells your 30-day story with numbers.
- Result to aim for: 200–500 real followers, 5–15 warm connections, one project, 30+ days of proof.
- Day 30: take your 30-day scorecard, your best post, your project, and your growth — and write the "Day 30 report." Then set the next 30.

## Rules of the Road

1. **No zero days.** Ever. Even 10 minutes counts.
2. **One focus.** If it doesn't feed coding or the online presence, it waits.
3. **Consistency beats talent.** Posting daily at a 6/10 beats posting weekly at a 9/10.
4. **People follow journeymen.** Show the struggle, the build, the numbers. Perfection is boring.
5. **The body is the anchor.** When you don't want to post, train. When you don't want to train, post. One discipline drags the other.
6. **Judge by day 30, not day 5.** Unstoppable = still standing when others quit.
