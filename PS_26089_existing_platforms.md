## 1. Urban Company

**URL:** urbancompany.com
**Type:** Private (VC-funded, IPO Sept 2025)
**Founded:** 2014
**HQ:** Gurgaon
**Partners:** 50,000+ gig professionals
**Revenue:** ₹1,555 Cr (FY26)

### Features
- Full-stack managed platform
- 247 training classrooms (17 cities)
- SOP-based service delivery
- Background checks
- Insurance coverage
- In-app safety buttons

### Drawbacks
| Issue | Impact |
|-------|--------|
| 25-30% commission | High cost for workers |
| No worker benefits | Gig = no EPF/ESIC |
| VC-profit driven | Wealth extraction |
| Limited to metros | Not rural/semi-urban |
| Surge pricing | Customer frustration |
| Worker exploitation claims | BBC reported issues |

### Verdict: **DIRECT COMPETITOR** – But high commission, no cooperative model

## Gap Analysis

**What existing platforms DON'T do:**
1. ❌ Cooperative ownership (workers own platform)
2. ❌ Low commission (5-10% vs 25-30%)
3. ❌ Social security (EPF/ESIC for gig workers)
4. ❌ Rural/semi-urban coverage
5. ❌ Government backing (Ministry of Cooperation)
6. ❌ Community wealth retention
7. ❌ Women worker safety focus

**PS 26089 USP:**
- **First cooperative-owned gig platform in India**
- Workers get fair wages + social security
- 5-10% commission (vs 25-30% private)
- Community wealth stays local
- Government backing = trust
- Rural coverage = massive market
