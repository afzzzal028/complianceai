---

## Recommendation

**PS 26089 (Cooperative Gig Platform) has highest potential** because:
- No direct competitor exists
- Government backing (Ministry of Cooperation)
- Massive market (₹22,000 Cr home services)
- Social impact angle = media attention
- Regulatory tailwind (gig worker protection laws)

**PS 26047 (Patient Case-Taking) is safest bet** because:
- A-HMIS already exists = government push
- Clear Ayush-specific gap
- ABDM integration = government support
- Lower competition

**PS 26044 (Academia-Industry) needs differentiation** because:
- Internshala/NCS are established
- College integration is hard to achieve
- Needs strong college partnerships
