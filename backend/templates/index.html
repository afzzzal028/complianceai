⚖️ Statutory Legal Metrology Guide
📜 Rule 6: Declarations on Packaged Goods
Mandates Name/Address of Manufacturer, Generic Name, Net Mass/Volume, Month/Year of Mfg, MRP (incl. of all taxes), Unit Sale Price (2022 amendment), and Consumer Care details.
⚠️ Section 36: Penalties for Non-Compliance
First Offense: Up to ₹25,000 fine.
Second Offense: Up to ₹50,000 fine.
Subsequent: Up to ₹1,00,000 fine or imprisonment up to 1 year.
🍏 FSSAI Packaging & Labelling Reg. 2020
14-digit FSSAI License, Veg/Non-Veg Logo (Green/Brown dot), and Expiry/Best Before duration are strictly mandatory on all food commodities.
